## 1. CUSTOMER FEATURES ENHANCED DATASET

**Records:** 1,077 customers (100% of available customers: 20% high-value, 60% mid-value, 20% low-value)

## Key Relationships and Usage Guidelines

### For Price Elasticity Modeling:
1. **Dependent Variable**: `SalesQty` (quantity demanded)
2. **Primary Independent Variable**: `UnitPrice` (price)
3. **Key Controls**: Customer context (RFM, loyalty), Product context (market position)
4. **Elasticity Proxies**: `PriceElasticityProxy`, `PriceVolatility`

### For Customer Segmentation:
1. **Primary Segmentation**: `RFMScore` (ready-to-use segments)
2. **Value Tiers**: `CustomerLifetimeValue`, `Monetary`
3. **Loyalty Indicators**: `CustomerLoyaltyScore`, `Frequency`, `Recency`

### For Product Performance:
1. **Revenue Leaders**: `TotalRevenue`, `RevenuePerCustomer`
2. **Market Position**: `CustomerPenetration`, `MarketShare`
3. **Demand Patterns**: `SalesFrequency`, `ProductDemandStrength`

### Data Quality Notes:
- **Missing Values**: Filled with 0 where appropriate
- **Sampling**: Customer data uses stratified sampling (20% high, 60% mid, 20% low value)
- **Date Range**: Covers full transaction history in source data
- **Calculations**: All ratios include +1 offsets to avoid division by zero

This comprehensive data dictionary enables precise feature selection and interpretation for elasticity modeling, customer segmentation, and business intelligence applications.
